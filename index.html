About the drill library

Match Fit builds sessions from one shared library of drills. It started with TSheehan's own U6 to U11 session plans and has grown with drills from the GAA Fun Do coaching resources (2007), the Nursery Programme, the Kellogg's GAA Cúl Camps hurling resource (Leinster GAA), Cavan GAA, a Monaghan drills pack and a football coaching manual by Philip Kerr (2004). Not every drill has been checked line by line, so use your own judgement. U10 and up get a warm-up, passing, tackling, attacking, sometimes possession, and a game, aimed at 60 minutes (use Adjust length to change it). U6 to U9 get a warm-up, skill games and team games, with no fixed length. Any drill can be shuffled, picked by hand, kept, reordered, edited or removed, and you can add your own. Save a session to plan it for a date: every coach on that team can open it from the Schedule and edit it. Run it times each drill with a beep and keeps the screen on. Share gives a short text for WhatsApp or Spond, a PDF, or a link for coaches, and Rate or review this session takes thirty seconds after training. Browse the Library tab to see everything and where each drill came from.
