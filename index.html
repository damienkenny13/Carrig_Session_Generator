Match Fit — GAA Session Builder (Underage)

Build a hurling or football training session for U6 through U18 in seconds — generate at random, hand-pick from the full library, then swap, reorder, filter by players and equipment on hand, or trim to a set length.

ℹ️ About this tool

This started as TSheehan's own U10 and U11 training session plans — 135 drills catalogued across two full seasons of real sessions this team has actually run, 36 of them with a layout diagram. It now also carries U10 and U12 technique drills from the official GAA Fun Do Football and Hurling Coaching Classes resources (2007) — 177 more, covering catching, kicking, striking, tackling, evasion and blocking, plus the fun games and modified games that go with each skill — all 177 with a layout diagram traced from the resource's own illustrations. It also carries 38 hurling entries for ages 11–13 from the Kellogg's GAA Cúl Camps Hurling Resource (Leinster GAA) — 30 day-session drills and games and 8 wall-ball sessions, all with a diagram — placed here alongside the U10/U12 material since the source doesn't split ages finer than 11–13. 251 of 350 drills in this mode now have a diagram. Filter by U10/U11/U12 to narrow to a specific age. U16/18 content has its own mode now — see the 13-18 tab above.

U10/U11/U12 mode: 350 drills — 135 from the team's own session library (source: TSheehan, U10/U11 only, 36 with a diagram), 177 U10/U12 technique drills and games from the official GAA Fun Do Football and Hurling Coaching Classes resources (2007, all with a diagram), and 38 hurling entries for ages 11–13 from the Kellogg's GAA Cúl Camps Hurling Resource (Leinster GAA — 30 day-session drills/games plus 8 wall-ball sessions, all with a diagram, placed here since the source doesn't split ages finer than 11–13) — 251 of 350 drills in this mode have a diagram. 13-18 mode: 46 drills/games (football-only, 43 with a diagram) — split out into its own mode so it's no longer mixed in with the U10/U11/U12 pool. 16 from Cavan GAA's Coaching & Games resource, plus 30 from a Philip Kerr (2004) football coaching manual (warm-ups, speed/agility, passing and evasion, tackling technique, shooting drills and tactical games for older players — 27 of the 30 with a diagram traced from the manual's own layouts). Same full-builder mechanics as U10/U11/U12 (55–65 min target, swap/reorder/edit/remove/add), and now has entries in every category including warm-up and possession.
U8/U9 mode: 294 drills — 131 from the club's own weekly session archive, 45 from the Monaghan Drills reference pack (football-only, with diagrams), 88 more from the U8 sections of the GAA Fun Do Coaching Classes resources (also all with diagrams), and 30 hurling drills and games for ages 8–10 from the Kellogg's GAA Cúl Camps Hurling Resource (all with a diagram) — 137 of 294 drills in this mode have a diagram. U6/U7 mode: 336 drills — 110 from the club's own weekly session archive, 196 fundamental-movement and ball-skill exercises from the GAA Fun Do Nursery Programme (16 with a diagram), and 30 hurling drills and games for ages 6–7 from the Kellogg's GAA Cúl Camps Hurling Resource (all with a diagram) — 46 of 336 drills in this mode have a diagram. Both younger modes are skills/games-led (warm-up → skill games → team games). None of the externally-sourced drills have been reviewed line-by-line the way the U10/U11 club library was — see Data Notes in the library for how each batch was extracted. Diagrams for the Coaching Classes, Monaghan, Nursery and Cúl Camps entries were traced from the real layouts in the source resources, not invented from the text alone. Same swap/reorder/edit/remove/add tools in all four modes, but only U10/U11/U12 and 13-18 modes have a fixed duration target — sessions vary more at younger ages.
